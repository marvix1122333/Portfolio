How I work

My design process.

Every great product follows a structured thinking path, from understanding the real problem to delivering something users love.

01
Discover

Deeply understand the problem: who the users are, what they need, and what’s blocking them, through empathy and research.

02
Define

Synthesize insights into clear problem statements, user flows, and information architecture before touching a single design frame.

03
Design

From wireframes to high-fidelity screens, craft interfaces that balance visual quality with usability, grounded in user needs.

04
Deliver

Polished prototypes, interaction specs, and design systems, handed off with clarity so developers build exactly what was designed.

Case Study · Mobile App

BladeRoom

AI-powered hairstyle discovery and booking platform where users scan their face, get personalised recommendations, and virtually try on styles before booking their hairstylist.

Why hairstyle booking is still guesswork

Finding the right hairstyle and hairstylist is mostly guesswork. People scroll through social media, show up to a salon, and hope the stylist understands their vision. There’s no easy way to visualise a style on your own face before committing, and no reliable platform combining discovery, personalisation, and booking in one place.

The Solution

BladeRoom combines AI-powered hairstyle recommendations with a full booking and marketplace platform. Users scan their face, receive personalised suggestions, then use the TryOn feature to virtually see how any style looks on them before booking.

Two user types, one platform

Regular users discover styles and book appointments. Hairstylists apply to join, get approved, and their interface adapts to reveal management tools, while keeping the familiar core experience intact. Both users share access to wallet, marketplace and product ordering.

Key Design Decisions

Leading with the AI feature

The AI face scan and TryOn is the most unique part of BladeRoom. I designed it as a primary action on the home screen so users immediately understand this isn’t just another booking app.

Permission-based interface upgrade

Instead of a separate app for hairstylists, BladeRoom gives them an interface upgrade once approved. The core feels familiar, but stylist-specific tools appear naturally, reducing confusion and making onboarding seamless.

Gloroq

Global gaming competition platform for gamers, creators and audiences: live streaming, team management, dual dashboards, and an accessibility-first color system built for long screen sessions.

Why this needed two dashboards, not one

Gaming competitions are fragmented, with no unified place to discover tournaments, join teams, or stream live events. There’s also a real accessibility problem: gamers spend hours on screen, but most platforms ignore the visual fatigue this causes, especially for users with color blindness.

The Solution

Gloroq is a desktop platform where anyone can explore live competitions, join or create teams, and switch into a Creator Dashboard to build and manage their own tournaments. The color system was designed specifically to reduce eye fatigue and remain legible for users with color blindness.

Dual dashboard system

Users have a standard audience/gamer dashboard. Creators switch into a separate creator mode with a distinct theme color, making it immediately clear which mode they’re in and preventing confusion between the two experiences.

UVS

Multi-entity barbering platform with three completely separate onboarding paths for clients, barbers and organisations, plus a full dark mode design system and admin dashboard.

Mobile AppDark ModeMulti-Entity

Platform

One platform, three completely different users

Barbering platforms typically serve one user type, the client. But the reality is more complex: individual clients, independent barbers, and organisations requesting group haircuts all have completely different needs and workflows.

The Solution

UVS solves this with three separate onboarding paths chosen at the very start of sign-up. Once a user selects their type, client, barber, or entity, they enter a completely tailored experience. Nothing overlaps. Each flow is designed specifically for that user’s real needs.

Dark mode design system

UVS was built with a full dark mode design system, not just a colour toggle, but a complete rethink of contrast, depth, and hierarchy for low-light environments. The admin dashboard extends this to desktop scale.
